Alternate moves, White first. Rooks slide orthogonally, bishops diagonally, queens both; sliders cannot jump. Knights jump in an L; kings move one square. Pawns advance one (two initially if clear), capture diagonally and promote to q/r/b/n. Never leave your king in check. Castling requires unmoved king/rook, clear intervening squares, and no check on the king's start/transit/destination. En passant captures a just-double-moved pawn, immediately only, without exposing your king.

Checkmate wins; stalemate/insufficient material draw. Claim a draw at three identical positions or 100 halfmoves without pawn move/capture: claim_draw, or claim_draw:<legal intended UCI> if that move would qualify. Claims do not move pieces. Fivefold/150 halfmoves draw automatically; checkmate wins precedence. Repetition includes turn, castling and legal en passant. Use only legal_actions. Examples: e2e4 e7e5 g1f3; a7a8n underpromotes. max_plies truncates separately.
